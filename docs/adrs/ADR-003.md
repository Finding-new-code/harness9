# ADR-003: Multi-Angle Editorial Scoring Matrix Architecture

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Deciders:** Head of Engineering, Editorial & Cognitive Team  
**Scope:** Editorial Intelligence Engine (`src/editorial/`)  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

Conventional automated video generation tools take a topic prompt (e.g. "Explain GPUs") and immediately generate a generic, Wikipedia-style explanatory script. These outputs lack:
1. **Editorial Tension**: No cognitive curiosity gap, surprising revelation, or distinct storytelling point-of-view.
2. **Audience-Topic Alignment**: No structured evaluation of whether the angle fits the target audience's technical depth.
3. **Multi-Angle Exploration**: Failure to consider alternative narrative angles (e.g. historical struggle vs modern economic impact vs contrarian mechanism).

---

## 2. Decision & Architecture

We decided to introduce a pre-scriptwriting **Editorial Intelligence Engine** (`src/editorial/`) that generates and evaluates candidate angles before script generation begins.

### 2.1 5 Angle Archetypes
For every `ResearchDossier`, the generator synthesizes 5 competing angles spanning distinct archetypes:
1. `contrarian`: Subverts widespread assumptions.
2. `deep_dive`: Explains core technical/physical mechanisms.
3. `data_led`: Centers on quantitative scaling laws and empirical data.
4. `human_narrative`: Biographical struggle, competition, and human drama.
5. `future_impact`: Projections and transformative paradigm shifts.

### 2.2 9-Dimension Scoring Matrix
Each angle is scored across 9 dimensions normalized to $[0.0, 1.0]$:
$$S = \sum_{i=1}^{8} w_i d_i + w_9 (1.0 - d_9)$$

| Dimension | Weight | Criteria |
|---|:---:|---|
| Audience Relevance | $0.15$ | Fits target viewer comprehension and interest. |
| Novelty | $0.15$ | Fresh, non-obvious insight. |
| Hook Potential | $0.15$ | Ability to arrest attention in opening 5 seconds. |
| Narrative Potential | $0.10$ | Natural 4-act progression and dramatic arc. |
| Creator Fit | $0.10$ | Conforms to Brand Constitution and creator strengths. |
| Evidence Availability | $0.10$ | Strongly backed by verified dossier claims. |
| Visual Potential | $0.10$ | Rich opportunities for dynamic diagrams and collages. |
| Platform Fit | $0.10$ | Optimized for platform format (16:9 or 9:16). |
| Saturation Risk | $0.05$ | Penalizes overused or cliché tropes (inverted). |

### 2.3 Winning Angle Selection & Narrative Planning
The engine selects the top-scoring angle ($a^* = \arg\max S(a_k)$), generates 3 hook variations, and constructs a 4-act `ContentOutline` that directly feeds the scriptwriter.

---

## 3. Consequences & Trade-Offs

### Positive Consequences
- **High Retention & Engagement**: Videos open with compelling cognitive hooks and deliberate narrative pacing.
- **Explainability**: Winning angle selection includes an itemized score matrix and selection rationale.
- **Editorial Diversity**: The 5 archetypes ensure varied coverage across multiple video productions.

### Negative Consequences / Mitigations
- **Additional LLM Inference Stage**: Adds one LLM call before scriptwriting.
  - *Mitigation*: The ideation call consumes $<1.5\text{k tokens}$ ($\approx \$0.01$) while dramatically improving video quality and viewer retention.
