# ADR-001: 17-State Lifecycle State Machine Architecture

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Deciders:** Head of Engineering, Architecture Team  
**Scope:** Core Orchestration (`src/orchestrator/state_machine.py`)  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

Early versions of video generation pipelines executed as linear, unmonitored sequential scripts. If a mid-pipeline stage failed (e.g. audio synthesis timeout or broken asset download), the entire workflow crashed without intermediate checkpointing, audit trails, or the ability to pause and resume. Furthermore, there were no structural guarantees preventing the system from attempting to render videos before research or scripts were validated.

The system required a robust, deterministic orchestration model capable of:
1. Enforcing strict, non-skipping stage progression.
2. Validating typed contract payloads at every stage boundary.
3. Providing an immutable audit trail of execution durations and data digests.
4. Supporting pauses for human review and safe error recovery.

---

## 2. Decision & Architecture

We decided to implement a **17-State Deterministic Lifecycle State Machine** (`ProductionStateMachine`) in `src/orchestrator/state_machine.py`.

### 2.1 The 17 Canonical Lifecycle States
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{CREATED} &\to \text{RESEARCH\_PLANNED} \to \text{RESEARCH\_IN\_PROGRESS} \to \text{RESEARCH\_COMPLETED} \\
&\to \text{EDITORIAL\_ANALYSIS} \to \text{ANGLE\_SELECTED} \to \text{OUTLINE\_APPROVED} \\
&\to \text{SCRIPTING\_IN\_PROGRESS} \to \text{SCRIPT\_COMPLETED} \\
&\to \text{VOICE\_GENERATED} \to \text{VOICE\_QA\_PASSED} \\
&\to \text{ASSETS\_DISCOVERED} \to \text{ASSETS\_FROZEN} \\
&\to \text{COMPOSITION\_GENERATED} \to \text{RENDER\_IN\_PROGRESS} \to \text{RENDER\_COMPLETED} \\
&\to \text{COMPLETED}
\end{aligned}$$

Plus 3 control and exception states: `PAUSED_FOR_HUMAN`, `FAILED`, and `CANCELLED`.

### 2.2 Transition Guards & Jump Rejection
- All permitted state transitions are explicitly registered in a lookup table `VALID_TRANSITIONS: Dict[ProductionState, Set[ProductionState]]`.
- Any attempt to execute an unregistered transition or illegal state jump (e.g. `CREATED` to `SCRIPT_COMPLETED`) raises `StateTransitionError` and aborts execution.
- Transitions must supply a validated Pydantic v2 payload corresponding to that lifecycle milestone.

### 2.3 Immutable Audit Logging
- Every transition appends a `TransitionRecord` containing:
  - `from_state` and `to_state`
  - UTC ISO timestamp
  - `payload_summary` (cryptographic SHA-256 digests and core metrics)
  - `duration_ms` (precise elapsed time)
- The audit trail is persisted atomically to `transition_history.json` and `transition_history.yaml`.

---

## 3. Consequences & Trade-Offs

### Positive Consequences
- **Complete Determinism**: Every production run follows a verifiable, repeatable sequence.
- **Flawless Resumability**: Paused workflows can be safely inspected and resumed from their last validated state.
- **Auditability**: Post-mortem debugging can inspect exact stage execution times and payload digests.

### Negative Consequences / Mitigations
- **Boilerplate Overhead**: Adding a new lifecycle state requires updating `VALID_TRANSITIONS` and unit tests.
  - *Mitigation*: The 17 canonical states represent the complete fundamental domain model of video production and are designed to be stable.
