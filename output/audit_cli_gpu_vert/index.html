The Chef vs. The Assembly Line

The Chef vs. The Assembly Line. If a CPU is a master chef preparing one complex dish at lighting speed, a GPU is a factory of ten thousand cooks chopping vegetables at once. A central processing unit (CPU) typically possesses between 8 and 64 high-speed cores designed for complex branching logic and low latency.

PART 02 • HARNESS 9

The Power of SIMD Parallelism

The Power of SIMD Parallelism. Originally designed to render 3D video game polygons, GPUs process massive arrays of pixels with a single broadcast instruction. Modern flagship GPUs feature over 16,000 arithmetic logic units (ALUs) executing parallel mathematical operations across high-bandwidth memory.

PART 03 • HARNESS 9

Fueling the AI Revolution

Fueling the AI Revolution. Because artificial intelligence calculations are essentially giant matrix multiplications, GPUs became the turbo-engine of modern deep learning. Matrix multiplication in neural networks maps directly to GPU tensor architectures, enabling trillion-parameter AI models to train efficiently.
